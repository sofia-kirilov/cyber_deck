Shell Trinket Compact
A seashell-shaped trinket compact with a round touchscreen in the lid. Designed in CadQuery (Python), based on my own sketch.
Size: about 82 × 90 × 46 mm closed
Shell: ribbed scallop shell with fine engraved lines, a relief ribbon bow, stars, a starfish, a pearl, an engraved signature, and pearl-beaded rims
Display: 1.28" round capacitive touchscreen (ESP32-S3) set into the lid, with LiPo battery and USB-C charging
Storage: trinket tray about 22 mm deep in the base
Closing: snap latch at the front and a snap-in hinge, no hardware needed
